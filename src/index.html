USGS terrain and every OpenStreetMap trail from Ramshead to Bear Mountain: 190+ trails, 15 lifts.

  • Drag left / right to carve
  • Push up to crouch and go faster, pull down to stand tall and slow
  • JUMP, then tap again in the air for up to two boosts
  • In the air: pull back to backflip, push forward to front flip
  • Double-swipe left or right for a 360. Land it straight or you'll eat it
