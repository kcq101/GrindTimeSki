# Sound sources

Every sound in the game is built from these recordings by `tools/sfx/build_sfx.py`. All are CC0 (public domain), so no credit is required, but here it is anyway. The originals are in `src/`.

| Game sound | Recording | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `glide` (skis on snow) | [Snowboard_Slide_Loop_Mono_02.wav](https://freesound.org/s/612664/) (Freesound HQ preview) | Nox_Sound | CC0 1.0 |
| `edge` (carving edge, tumble slide, snow off branches) | [Snowboard_Slide_Loop_Mono_01.wav](https://freesound.org/s/612665/) (Freesound HQ preview) | Nox_Sound | CC0 1.0 |
| `wind` | [Car Strong Wind Noise.mp3](https://freesound.org/s/439242/), 10–40 s, above 500 Hz only (the air, not the car) | FunWithSound | CC0 1.0 |
| `crunch1`–`5` (landings, bumps, falls, pole plants) | [snow footsteps](http://freesound.org/people/Ryding/sounds/94337/), as cut for minetest_game (`default_snow_footstep.*.ogg`) | Ryding | CC0 1.0 |
| `chop1`, `chop2` (tree hits, a ski knocking) | [wood chops](https://freesound.org/people/Sheyvan/sounds/476113/), as cut for minetest_game (`default_dig_choppy.*.ogg`) | Sheyvan | CC0 1.0 |
| `clank1`, `clank2` (chair passing a lift tower) | [wood and metal sound effects vol. 2](http://opengameart.org/content/wood-and-metal-sound-effects-volume-2), as cut for minetest_game (`default_place_node_metal.*.ogg`) | Ogrebane | CC0 1.0 |

The Nox_Sound and FunWithSound recordings were found through the Winter Sled Valheim mod (github.com/rgeraskin/valheim-winter-sled), which credits them the same way.
